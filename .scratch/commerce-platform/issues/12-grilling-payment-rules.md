# Payment gateway shortlist and payment rules

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Question

1. **Gateways at launch.** Which one or two, and one active with another on standby?
2. **Payment states and timeouts.** How long an unpaid order holds stock; double payment; abandoned payment; reconciliation with the gateway's own reports.
3. **Refunds.** The process outside the system and what the system records.
4. **Settlement and fees.** What the store must track or show.
5. **Gateway down.** What the customer sees and what staff can do.

## Context

- Online card payment through a gateway only (owner decision). Read the payment gateways research first.
