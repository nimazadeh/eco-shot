# Inventory and stock rules

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 06, 07

## Question

1. Stock is tracked per variant: confirm, and how physical stock, code stock and files differ.
2. **Reservation.** Does checkout hold stock, for how many minutes, and when is it released?
3. **Oversell and backorder policy.**
4. **Low-stock alerts:** to whom, at what level.
5. **Code stock running out:** does the product become unavailable automatically?
6. **Adjustments.** Manual stock changes and their audit trail.

## Context

- Terms defined so far: Code stock, Digital code.
- From the tax and law research: selling stock that is not there triggers an immediate-refund duty (article 39), which makes reservation and oversell rules a legal as well as a business matter.
- From the catalog model decisions: stock is per variant (physical stock, or code stock for digital variants); out of stock is derived and is not a product state.
