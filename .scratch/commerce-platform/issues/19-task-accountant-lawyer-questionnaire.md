# Questionnaire for the owner's accountant and lawyer: VAT, e-invoices and digital goods

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Using `to-questionnaire`, turn the six questions at the end of `research/tax-and-law.md` into a questionnaire the owner can hand to their accountant and lawyer, and record when it is sent and what comes back. The tickets on money rules, checkout, digital delivery and orders proceed in the meantime with explicit, provisional assumptions and are revisited when the answers arrive.

## Context

- Human in the loop: the owner sends it. It does not block other tickets, because waiting weeks for outside answers would stall Phase 1; decisions that depend on it are marked provisional in their tickets.
- Questions cover: VAT status and the 1405 rate and price display; Moodian e-invoice duties at the expected turnover; whether instantly delivered digital codes and files are exempt from the 7-day withdrawal right; the content of the pre-contract information and its confirmation; whose name the domain, Enamad, bank account and tax file must carry; record retention and privacy duties.
- No legal or tax compliance is claimed anywhere in this project.

## Inputs

- Skill: `to-questionnaire` (grill the send: who it goes to and what the owner needs back).
- Research: `research/tax-and-law.md`.

## Done when

A questionnaire file exists, the owner has sent it (or decided who will), and the answer records what came back or when it is expected.
