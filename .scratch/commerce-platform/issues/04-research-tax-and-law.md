## Answer

Findings with a source and an evidence label per claim: [research/tax-and-law.md](../research/tax-and-law.md). Not legal or tax advice; the statute text was read in reproductions (the ILO's copy and a legal news site), because the official sites could not be fetched.

- **Withdrawal right (Electronic Commerce Law, articles 37 and 38):** at least 7 working days, counted from delivery for goods; the supplier refunds the exact amount; the buyer pays only the return shipping; the period starts only after the supplier gave the required information (articles 33 to 35).
- **Out of stock after payment (article 39):** refund immediately unless the buyer agrees to wait. This governs the code-stock-runs-out case.
- **Exceptions (Cabinet regulation of 1383):** services started with consent, daily consumables, market-priced goods, personalised or non-returnable goods, **opened packaged software**, newspapers. **Digital codes and downloadable files are not named**, so their status is a question for a lawyer.
- **VAT:** 9 percent in the 1400 law, 10 percent for 1403 per a tax-administration notice (secondary); the 1405 rate is unconfirmed. **Moodian e-invoice duties** were found only in secondary sources and are unverified.
- **Enamad:** required by Torob and Emalls; conditions are from secondary guides.
- **Docs needed and six questions for the owner's accountant and lawyer** are listed in the findings file.
