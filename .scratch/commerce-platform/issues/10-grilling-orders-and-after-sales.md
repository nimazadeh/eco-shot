# Orders, cancellations, returns, warranty, support and SMS notifications

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 07

## Question

1. **Statuses.** The order and payment statuses that customers and staff see.
2. **Cancellation.** Rules before payment, after payment, and before shipment.
3. **Returns and refunds** for physical products (use the legal facts from the research) and the manual refund workflow.
4. **Warranty** for electronics: what is shown, how a claim is handled (inside the system or outside).
5. **Support.** Which channel (contact form, phone, tickets) and what staff see.
6. **Notifications.** Which events send an SMS, weighing cost against value.

## Context

- Refunds are done by staff outside the system at first (owner decision); the system records them.
- From the Torob and Emalls research: Torob's terms require sending the order number and an exact invoice to the buyer's email and mobile after ordering, tracking by order number on the shop's site, dispatch within 3 business days by default, and answering order follow-ups and complaints. Decide the invoice channel (SMS link, email, both).
